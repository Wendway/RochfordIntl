Global Gas

The explosive growth in liquefied-natural-gas (LNG) trade, led by the U.S., now the world’s largest exporter, and the opening of historic market monopolies has transformed a largely regional business into a global commodity, involving multiple types of players. We help gas companies create distinctive outcomes across the energy value chain and in every region.

Utility System Transformation

The delivery of reliable, affordable energy is transforming into a system that is much more networked and distributed. We are working side-by-side with utilities to secure the next generation grid, enable new business models, meet fast-growing demand from data centers and electrification, and transition to the utility of the future.

We help energy companies and investors build low-carbon strategies that create value: decarbonizing existing operations, evaluating carbon capture, hydrogen and renewable fuels, and allocating capital to the new energy businesses that fit their strengths.
