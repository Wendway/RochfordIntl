About Rochford Strategies

An independent energy consulting and market intelligence firm headquartered in New York.

Who We Are

Independent advice for a complex energy industry

Rochford Strategies (“RS”) advises oil and gas producers, midstream and downstream operators, utilities, renewable energy developers and the investors who finance them. We combine the strategic perspective of a management consultancy with the market depth of a dedicated research house.

Our consultants have spent their careers inside the industry – on trading desks, in operations and planning teams, and in investment banking – so we understand both the numbers and the realities behind them.

Because we are independent, our advice is shaped by one objective: helping clients make better decisions.

Our Approach
