RS Dashboard

Delivers top-trade-desk insights into the short-term supply, demand, and pricing dynamics of the U.S. oil and gas market. Driven by daily observations of fundamental data, including storage flows and electric-power burns, RS advisory service provides clients with actionable insights for your oil and gas price exposure.

RSCast report is an annual publication that provides a uniquely integrated analysis of the U.S. energy sector. The report is an invaluable reference tool, offering supply, demand and price forecasts for each of the fossil fuels – natural gas, coal, and crude oil – through 2040. It also covers the entire electric power sector, offering power price forecasts at a national level, as well as for each individual power market. In more recent versions, forecasts for renewable energy and environmental/emission allowance markets have been added, as well.

RS LNG Outlook offers subscribers a deep dive into the rapidly changing and often opaque global LNG industry. Special focus is given to the ongoing development of U.S. LNG export projects, but global issues – including shifting pricing dynamics – are explored in depth, as well. The Outlook includes short-term, country-by-country supply and demand forecasts, as well as insight into the main trends impacting each region. Subscribers also gain access to RS’s comprehensive LNG cloud database, which includes detailed information on every existing, under construction, and proposed LNG import and export project in the world.

Provides the outlook for gas supply and demand fundamentals, and prices, for the current year plus the next two years. Consists of four Quarterly Gas Reports and eight Monthly Gas Reports. The quarterly reports provide a comprehensive and granular assessment of the industry, which includes details such as a forecast of drilling activity.

RS Petroleum Report provides RS’s view of fundamentals for the global market, including supply, demand, trade and price, and is published monthly. On an annual basis, the report includes a comprehensive assessment and outlook for the current year and the next two years, with ten monthly updates, and one report consisting of a comprehensive 20-year outlook.

Exploration & Production

The drastic decline in commodity prices raises significant issues for exploration and production (“E&P”) companies, which are further exacerbated by global supply and demand imbalances, geopolitical issues and climate change concerns.

To protect enterprise value through this challenging climate, E&P companies must successfully navigate:

  • Plugging and Abandonment Costs
  • Variance in Federal and State Laws and Regulations
  • Reserve Reports Uncertainties
  • Hedging
  • Borrowing Base Considerations
